Your privacy

Glyde is built to keep your browsing yours. Here is what that means.

  • No telemetry. Glyde does not send usage data, crash reports or studies.
  • No adverts or sponsored shortcuts. Nothing is paid to appear in your browser.
  • Tracking protection on. Strict blocking, HTTPS only mode and Global Privacy Control are on by default.
  • Update checks. Glyde asks GitHub whether a newer version exists. You can turn this off in settings.
  • Google is the only search engine. Google sees what you search for, under its own privacy policy. Glyde cannot hide that.
  • Private windows leave no history on this device. Websites and your network can still see your activity.
