Cirkle ID

Sign in with your Cirkle ID to keep your look and layout settings the same on every computer. They are encrypted before they leave this one.

This passphrase locks your settings. It is kept only on this computer, so Cirkle ID cannot read them. If you lose it, the stored copy cannot be recovered, only replaced.
