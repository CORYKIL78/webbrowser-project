The short version

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

1. This website

The Glyde website is made of plain pages. It does not use cookies, local storage or tracking scripts, so there is nothing to accept or refuse. Our hosting provider keeps standard server records, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

If we ever add anything that needs a cookie, we will update this page and ask for your permission first unless the cookie is strictly necessary for the site to work.

2. Cookies inside the Glyde browser

Websites you visit with Glyde can set cookies. That is controlled by you and by the browser, not by us.

  • Glyde is set up to block known trackers and to keep each website's cookies separate from other websites, so a tracker cannot follow you from site to site.
  • You can see, delete and block cookies in the browser's settings, and you can browse in a private window that forgets cookies when you close it.
  • Glyde sends a signal asking websites not to sell or share your data (Global Privacy Control). Websites are not always required to respect it.
