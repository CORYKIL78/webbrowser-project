This policy applies to our online services: Cirkle ID sign in, sync and the Szimon assistant. It is part of the Terms of Service. Browsing the web with the Glyde software is your own responsibility and is covered by the Terms of Use.

You must not

  • Break the law, or help anyone else to
  • Use the services to harass, threaten, deceive or abuse other people
  • Create, store or share illegal content, or any sexual content involving children (we report this to the authorities)
  • Use the Szimon assistant to produce malware, to plan harm, or to pretend to be a real person in order to mislead others
  • Try to get into accounts, data or systems that are not yours
  • Overload the services, scan them for weaknesses without our written permission, or get around usage limits
  • Collect data from the services automatically (scraping), or sell or share your access
  • Upload viruses or other harmful code
  • Pretend to be us or someone else

Security researchers

If you find a security problem, please tell us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} before making it public, and give us reasonable time to fix it. We will not take action against people who report problems in good faith, do not access other people's data, and do not disrupt the service.

What we may do

If this policy is broken we may remove content, limit or suspend access, close an account, or pass information to the authorities where the law requires or allows it. Where we reasonably can, we will warn you first.

Reporting a problem
