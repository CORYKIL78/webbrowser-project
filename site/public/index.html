What we are building

Glyde is in development and nothing here is released yet. This is the plan, and each item is marked with where it stands.

In development

Private by default

No telemetry, no adverts, tracking protection on, and secure connections preferred. Your browsing history stays on your device.

In development

Google search built in

One search engine, built into the address bar. Search suggestions are off by default so nothing is sent while you type.

Planned

Tabs your way

Put your tabs on the top, bottom, left or right, or in a floating panel. Built on the vertical tabs Firefox already has.

Planned

Split screen

Two pages side by side in one window, using Firefox's split view as the base.

Planned

Customise everything

Themes, colours, spacing and animations, all in a settings page that opens as a tab inside the browser.

Planned

Sync only you can read

Sign in with Cirkle ID and sync settings, bookmarks and passwords. Data is locked on your device before it is uploaded.

Planned

Szimon assistant

An optional assistant in a side panel. It only sees what you type, unless you choose to share a page with it.

Planned

Open source

The source will be published when the browser is ready for the public.

iPhone and iPad

Privacy in plain words

Glyde does not collect your browsing history or send usage reports. Searches go to Google, because that is the search engine built in, and Google's own privacy policy covers them. Read the full Privacy Policy.
